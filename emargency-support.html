What to Do in an Emergency

Follow these basic safety steps when possible.

01

Move to a Safer Place

Move toward a public, well-lit or secure location if it is safe to do so.

02

Call Emergency Services

Call 999 if you face immediate danger or urgently need police, fire or ambulance help.

03

Contact Someone You Trust

Inform a trusted family member, friend or nearby person about your situation.

04

Share Your Location Safely

If possible, share your location with someone you trust so they can find you.

05

Seek Further Support

Contact the relevant helpline for additional guidance and assistance.

Your safety comes first. Only share personal information with trusted people or authorized emergency service providers.
