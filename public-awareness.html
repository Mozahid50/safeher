Your Everyday Safety Checklist

Follow these simple habits to improve your everyday safety.

Keep trusted contacts easily accessible.
Protect passwords and personal information.
Stay aware of your surroundings while travelling.
Preserve relevant evidence of harassment.
Respect personal boundaries and consent.
Seek trusted support whenever you feel unsafe.
